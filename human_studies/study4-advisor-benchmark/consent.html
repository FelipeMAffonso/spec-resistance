Study Title: "Consumer Decision Making Study"

Background Information

We are inviting you to be part of a research study about how consumers evaluate product information and make purchasing decisions. We seek to understand how people use product comparison information to choose products. This form will tell you what the study is about, what we will ask you to do, and how your information will be used. Please read this carefully. Feel free to ask questions if you don't understand something. Taking part in this study is completely your choice. You can decide not to join, or if you join, you can stop at any time. There is no punishment or penalty if you decide not to take part or if you quit later.

This study is being conducted by:

This study is being done by Felipe M. Affonso from School of Marketing and International Business at Oklahoma State University.

Procedures

If you decide to join, we will ask you to:

Fill out a survey about consumer product evaluation and decision making. You will state your product preferences, view product comparison information, make a product selection, and answer questions about your decision.

It will take about 6 minutes.

Benefits and Risks of being in the Study

You may not get direct benefits from this study, but your participation could help researchers learn more about consumer decision-making and help others in the future.

This study involves the following risks:

There are no risks in this study that are greater than what you experience in daily life.

We will take steps to reduce these risks by:

If any question makes you uncomfortable, you can skip it or choose to quit the study at any time.

Will You get Paid?

You will be paid for taking part in the study. You will get compensation after you finish the study. You will be paid $0.20 per minute ($1.20 for a 6-minute survey). If the research team discovers that participants falsified their identity to meet eligibility criteria, the team reserves the right to dismiss the participant from the study and not pay them for participation.

Prolific does not allow for prorated compensation. In the event of an incomplete submission, you must contact the research team, and compensation will be determined based on what was completed and at the researchers' discretion.

PLEASE NOTE: This study contains a number of checks to make sure that participants are finishing the tasks honestly and completely. We are following Prolific's policies. As long as you read the instructions and complete the tasks, your submission will be approved. If you fail these checks, your submission will be rejected.

How Will Your Information Be Kept Private?

The information you share in this study will be anonymous. This means your name will not be collected or connected to your answers. Once you finish the study, we will not be able to remove your data because it will not be linked to you.

Your Prolific ID will be used to distribute payment to you, but will not be stored with the research data we collect from you.

The research team will do its best to keep your information private, using the technology available. However, because you are answering online, there is a small chance that someone who is not supposed to could see your answers. This risk is similar to what you might face when using the internet in daily life. If you have concerns, you can check the privacy policy of the online provider here: www.qualtrics.com/privacy-statement/

How Your Information Will Be Collected and Stored?

We will collect your information using surveys using the Qualtrics survey software. Your information will be stored in:

- Qualtrics Servers (encrypted and password-protected)

- Local data files (encrypted and password-protected on a hard drive inside the University)
